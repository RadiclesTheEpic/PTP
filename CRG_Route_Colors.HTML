So What Do The colors mean?

Columbus Rock Gym

Quick discaimer: some routes may be tagged wrong, and some climbing styles will do really good with certin routes. So these are not hard and fast rules but guidlines.

White

Routes ment for absolue begginers or a warm up, the easiest in the gym.

Purple

Routes for people that can do white tags and want to start improving.

Blue

This is for people who are stazrting to understand how to climb and want more.

Red

Now we are getting to the hard stuff, it takes most people a long time coming to the gym to be able to climb a red.

Orenge

The hardest routes in the gym, people can come for years and never be able to climb an Orenge tag.

Black

Unknown grade or just hidden, normaly super weird and very fun.
